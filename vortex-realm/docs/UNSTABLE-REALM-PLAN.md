# Unstable Realm — season plan (draft)

An original, story-driven chaos season for the Vortex Realm SMP, inspired by the *style* of
roleplay SMPs (one-of-a-kind characters, lore events, a world that reacts). Everything here is
our own: characters, map and story. It does not copy any existing SMP's creators, map or plot.

## 1. One-of-a-kind characters
- `/character` opens the roster. Each character can be owned by **one player only**; once taken it is gone.
- If the owner dies for good (final life) the character is "retired" and can return as a story NPC.
- Every character has: a passive, an active ability (cooldown), a weakness, a starting item and a story goal.

## 2. AI fighters and story NPCs
- Fighter bots that patrol, duel and raid, scaled to the strongest players.
- Story NPCs with memory: they remember who helped or betrayed them and change their dialogue, prices and loyalty.
- Built on our existing combat/NPC code; no real people are impersonated.

## 3. A world that reacts
- **Story acts:** the season runs in chapters (Arrival → Factions → Betrayal → War → Finale). Each chapter unlocks when the server reaches goals (bosses killed, alliances formed, votes).
- **Choices matter:** server-wide votes and key player actions decide the branch (for example who rules the capital, whether the rift is sealed or opened).
- **Events:** rifts, meteor nights, the jester's carnival, siege nights, and a final boss.

## 4. The map
- A new handcrafted world: central capital, 6 regions (frozen peaks, rift wastes, sky islands, sunken city, carnival grounds, ancient forest), all made with our build tools.
- Claims, homes and the market keep working.
