## 2. Staff ranks — what each does

| Rank | In game | Duties | Promotion after |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Helper** (trial, 2 weeks) | `practice.staff` (sees invisible players, staff chat), mute | Answer questions, watch chat, mute for chat rules, open tickets for reports | 4–6 weeks active, 0 abuse, good ticket ratings |
| **Moderator** | + `practice.staff.mod` (kick, temp-ban, screenshares `/ss`), `/alts` | Handle reports and tickets, screenshare suspects, temp bans | 2–3 months, trusted judgement |
| **Sr. Moderator** | + permanent bans, appeals | Train Helpers, review appeals, check moderator logs weekly | 3+ months, leads well |
| **Admin** | + `practice.admin` (`/pvpadmin`) | Server settings, kits, arenas, ranks, events setup | picked by the owner |
| **Manager** | everything but the store and money | Runs the staff team: hiring, promotions, weekly meeting, staff reviews | owner's right hand |
| **Builder** | creative build server / `/deco`, `/holo` | Arenas, lobby upgrades, event maps | portfolio based |
| **Event Host** | `/event`, tournaments | Runs 2+ events a week, writes brackets, announces winners | by activity |
| **Developer** | console / test server | Plugin config, website, bots | trial project |

**Staff rules** (pin in `#staff-chat`): treat everyone the same, punish with proof (screenshot / clip / log), never punish friends' enemies, no staff power in the SMP (the SMP is "everyone equal" — 3.11.0 enforces it), 7-day inactivity notice, staff never sell items or ranks.

**Punishment guide** (short): chat spam → 10m mute; toxicity → 1h mute → 1d → 3d; advertising → 7d ban; cheating (proven) → 30d ban, second time permanent; ban evasion → permanent on all accounts (`/alts`).

## 3. Applications

### Requirements

| Role | Age | Playtime | Other |
|---|---|---|---|
| Helper | 14+ | 24 h on the server, 2 weeks since first join | No punishments in 60 days, Discord linked, a working mic for the interview |
| Moderator | 15+ | from Helper only (no direct applications) | — |
| Builder | 13+ | — | Portfolio: 3+ builds (screenshots / world download), knows WorldEdit |
| Event Host | 14+ | 12 h | Ran or helped with events before |
| Developer | 15+ | — | GitHub / past work, a small trial task |
| Media / Creator | — | — | YouTube 500+ subs **or** TikTok 2,000+ followers **or** Twitch 50+ avg viewers; 1 video / stream on the server every month |

### Helper application questions
1. Minecraft name, age, time zone, languages.
2. How many hours a week can you be active? When?
3. Any staff experience? (server, role, how long, why you left)
4. Why do you want to be staff on [YourServer]?
5. Someone keeps calling another player names in chat. What do you do, step by step?
6. A friend of yours is reported for cheating with a clip. What do you do?
7. A player says staff banned them unfairly. What do you do?
8. Is there anything you'd improve on the server?
9. Do you have a working mic for a short voice interview?

### Process
1. Forms open in `#apply` (only when you need staff — keep it closed otherwise).
2. Sr. Moderator + Manager read every form within 7 days; reject / accept in `#application-results` (no reasons in public).
3. 10-minute voice interview: 3 situations to handle.
4. Helper trial: 2 weeks, a mentor (a Moderator), weekly check.
5. After the trial: kept or not, decided by Manager.

## 6. Upgrades to beat MCPVP, PvP Club and PvPHQ

What the big practice servers do well: low ping in many regions, very fast queues, beautiful arenas and lobbies, polished animations, fair ranked, strong anticheat, weekly tournaments with prizes, creators playing on it.

### Must-have (first month)
1. **Hosting in 2+ regions** (NA East + EU) — the plugin already supports nearest-region servers. Ping is the #1 reason players stay. Get at least 6–8 GB RAM per practice server (the current low-memory host is the main limit).
2. **Anticheat:** Grim (free) or Vulcan/Polar (paid). Hook it into the plugin's Sentinel and auto-ban only on high confidence.
3. **Fast queues at launch:** open few kits (5–6 most played) so queues fill; add more as players grow. Bots fill empty queues (`/bot`).
4. **Discord + DiscordSRV** linked, with reports, appeals and changelog working on day 1.
5. **Website** with leaderboards and profiles (the plugin's web panel), store, rules, vote links.
6. **Server lists:** Minecraft-MP, TopG, minecraftservers.org, planetminecraft — vote rewards in game (a cosmetic or shards).

### Next (months 2–3)
7. **Weekly tournaments** (Saturday NA, Sunday EU) with real prizes (store credit, exclusive wings).
8. **Creator program** (requirements above): a Media rank, their own cosmetic, a code in the store.
9. **Bedrock players** with Geyser + Floodgate (huge free audience on phones/consoles).
10. **Clans** with clan ELO and clan wars; **parties** 2v2/3v3 ranked.
11. **More arenas every month** (we are at 13 court arenas + vanilla maps; add 2 a month and retire the least-played).
12. **Lobby upgrade** with the same detail as the new arenas (next release).

### To stand out
13. **Pets, shoulder pets and wings unlocked by playtime** (3.11.0) — keeps players coming back daily; add seasonal colours.
14. **Tier tests streamed** on Twitch / TikTok live — free content.
15. **Short-form content:** 3 TikToks / YouTube Shorts a week: best clips, "rate my arena", 1v1 the owner.
16. **Events nobody else has:** SMP seasons with stories, bot-boss raids, "kit of the week" with double ELO.
17. **Staff quality:** tickets answered in < 12 h, appeals in < 48 h, a public punishment policy.

### Store and the Minecraft EULA (important)
Mojang's rules: you may sell **cosmetics** (wings colours, pets, name colours, chat tags, kill effects) but **not gameplay advantages** (better kits, more health, items that help win). Keep ranks cosmetic + convenience only (queue priority is allowed by most servers but keep it small). Read the current *Minecraft Usage Guidelines* before opening the store.
