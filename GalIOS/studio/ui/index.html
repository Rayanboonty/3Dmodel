GalaxyIOS Studio

iOS 27 on your Galaxy

One button does it all: Ubuntu, your iOS 27 image, the phone. Steps already done are skipped, and when it needs you (plug in the phone, start root) it waits and carries on by itself.

Do everything

Press once, then leave the PC on. The image build takes 1–3 hours.

    This PC

    Phone

    Step 1

    Build the image

    OneiOS's scripts turn Apple's iOS 27.0.1 for iPhone 17 Pro Max into the image the phone boots. It runs in Ubuntu inside Windows (WSL, installed by Studio), takes 1–3 hours and needs about 75 GB free on drive C:. Stop any time; building again resumes where it stopped.

    Ubuntu on this PC

    iOS files

    Choose your iPhone18,2_27.0.1_24A446_Restore.ipsw (any name is fine). With none chosen, the build reads the parts it needs straight from Apple. Either way the firmware is read once: later runs reuse it.

    The finished image

    Step 2

    Phone

    Connect the S25 Ultra by USB with USB debugging on, and start your temporary root. Studio finds root by itself: su over USB, or through the GalaxyIOS app when your root app grants apps only.

    Connection

    Root and tools

    Copy the image

    Puts the finished image in the phone's Download folder, where GalaxyIOS finds it (one tap: Import). Or add it from here on the Boot page.

    iPhone screen features

    Tells iOS it has the iPhone's OLED panel, Dynamic Island and rounded corners, from the device tree the image build read from your IPSW. That is what the always-on display and the Dynamic Island need. Do everything turns it on; turn it off here if iOS behaves worse. Takes effect at the next boot.

    Step 3

    Boot

    Uses the OneOS app's iOS 27 engine already on the phone (Studio adapts its phone check for the S25 Ultra) and puts everything in its stage, straight from this PC.

    If the way back fails its checks, the phone restarts into One UI; temporary root is gone after a restart.
