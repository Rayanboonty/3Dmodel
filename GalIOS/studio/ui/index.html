GALIOS STUDIO

iOS 27 on your Galaxy

One button does it all: Ubuntu, your iOS 27 image, the phone. Steps already done are skipped, and when it needs you (plug in the phone, start root) it waits and carries on by itself.

Do everything

Press once, then leave the PC on. The image build takes 1–3 hours.

    This PC

    Phone

    STEP 1

    Build the image

    OneiOS's scripts turn Apple's iOS 27.0.1 for iPhone 17 Pro Max into the image the phone boots. It runs in Ubuntu inside Windows (WSL, installed by Studio), takes 1–3 hours and needs about 75 GB free on drive C:. Stop any time; building again resumes where it stopped.

    Ubuntu on this PC

    iOS files

    Studio looks for iPhone18,2_27.0.1_24A446_Restore.ipsw in Downloads, Desktop, Documents and your other drives, and opens it to check it is the right one. Without it, the build reads the parts it needs straight from Apple.

    The finished image

    STEP 2

    Phone

    Connect the S25 Ultra by USB with USB debugging on, and start your temporary root. Studio finds root by itself: su over USB, or through the GalIOS app when your root app grants apps only.

    Connection

    Root and tools

    Copy the image

    Puts the finished image in the phone's Download folder, where GalIOS finds it (one tap: Import). Or add it from here on the Boot page.

    STEP 3

    Boot

    Uses the OneOS app's iOS 27 engine already on the phone (Studio adapts its phone check for the S25 Ultra) and puts everything in its stage, straight from this PC.
