GalaxyIOS 1.25 and GalaxyIOS Studio 1.16

**Your own picture on the Boot card.** Tap the phone drawing next to "iOS 27" and choose any picture from your phone (PNG with a transparent background looks best): GalaxyIOS keeps its own copy and shows it there instead of the drawing. Tap it again to choose another; press and hold to go back to the drawing.

Studio has the same: click the drawing on its Boot card and choose a picture on your PC; "Use the drawing" under the version line puts the drawing back.

Your picture stays on your phone and your PC. It is never part of GalaxyIOS, its repository or its releases, so you can use any picture you like for yourself.

Also in this line: GalaxyIOS (1.24), frosted glass and the floating tab bar (1.24), "bone and ink" (1.23), the volume keys set the speaker's level (runtime galios.22). Get the newest runtime in Setup, step 3.

Both the app and Studio update themselves (the app with root, Studio when no task runs). If you still have version 1.17 or older, install this one once with Studio (Phone, Install GalaxyIOS).
